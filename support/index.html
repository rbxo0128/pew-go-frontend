Reporting a bug

Including the following helps us fix things faster:

  • Device model and Android version
  • The screen or chapter where it happened
  • The steps that led to the problem
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Deleting your game data

Game data is stored only on your device. Clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game to delete it.
